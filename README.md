# PM Productivity Tools

Simple, no-dependency tools built for product managers to use in their daily workflows.

## Tools

### [Market & Customer Insights Generator](tools/market-insights/index.html)

A self-guided wizard that walks you through 5 stages of strategic thinking about your product and market:

1. **Product Context** -- ground your thinking in the problem and customer
2. **Market Landscape** -- map competitors, alternatives, and trends
3. **Differentiation** -- articulate what's actually different and why customers care
4. **Moats & Defensibility** -- stress-test what keeps competitors from catching up
5. **Success Metrics** -- define what winning looks like and what would make you pivot

Outputs a formatted markdown summary you can copy or download.

**How to use:** Open `tools/market-insights/index.html` in any browser. No setup, no accounts, no API keys.

## Philosophy

- Zero dependencies, runs in a browser
- Frameworks that force rigorous thinking, not just templates to fill in
- Built by a PM, for PMs
