Role Context

Ground yourself in the basics of your new role. This context shapes everything that follows.

In 1-2 sentences, what does this team exist to do?
What are you most uncertain about in this new role?

Stakeholder Ecosystem

Map the people who matter most to your success. Think broadly: your manager, skip-level, engineering and design leads, cross-functional partners, exec sponsors, and customer-facing teammates.

Goal Alignment

Connect your work to the goals around you. Misalignment here is the #1 reason PMs struggle in new roles.

What are the key objectives for your team this quarter or half?
What does your manager and their leadership chain care about most?
What company-level goals does your work ladder up to?
Any tension between team goals, leadership priorities, and business objectives?

Leadership & Business Context

Understand the power structure and business landscape you're operating in.

List from your direct manager up to the exec level
What themes come up repeatedly in all-hands, emails, strategy docs?
What are the big bets or strategic pillars for the org?
What metrics does the business track most closely?
Reorgs, strategy shifts, leadership changes, recent launches or failures

Team & Org Motivations

Understanding what drives people is as important as understanding the business. This is how you build trust fast.

What energizes the people you work with? Autonomy, impact, craft, recognition?
How does the team work together? What's the decision-making style?
Unwritten rules, communication preferences, meeting culture
What have you heard about what trips people up?
